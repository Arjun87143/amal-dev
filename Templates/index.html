About Me

The Man Behind The Frame

I'm Amal Dev — a video editor, musician, and visual storyteller. I don't just cut footage; I sculpt emotion from raw moments and turn them into cinematic experiences that linger long after the screen goes dark.

With a deep passion for film language, color theory, and rhythm, every edit I make is intentional. Every cut, every beat, every frame — a decision that serves the story.

Off the timeline, I'm living another story — strumming guitar strings, singing, and performing on stage as part of my band. Music and motion are my twin languages.
